## Caveats on these numbers

- **Verify in the cart.** These come from comparison sites (TLD-List, tldes) and
  registrar pages via search, not from live lookups — egress here is search-only.
  Prices update hourly and promos rotate.
- **VAT.** The registry fee is EUR-denominated and several of these are EU
  registrars. Netim's $21.50 is explicitly ex-VAT; an EU consumer adds ~20-22%.
  Non-EU buyers generally do not.
- **FX.** The registry charges EUR 10; every USD figure above is a conversion that
  moves with the euro.
- **Netim's "free first year" is a trap at 5 years** — $0 up front, $86/name by
  year five, the worst outcome in the table.
- **Site.eu advertises EUR 9.69**, below the EUR 10 registry fee, so it is a
  first-year loss-leader; its renewal rate is what matters and I could not confirm it.

## Which term to actually buy

1-year. The 5-year column exists because you asked, but pre-paying five years
hedges a price increase that this registry has not made in twenty years (EUR 8
from 2015, EUR 10 since 2023), while giving up the option to walk away. On the
26-name basket that is **$299 at risk instead of $1,496** — and on a speculative
basket you will want to drop most of these at the first renewal.

Buy 5 years only for the two or three you would keep regardless of whether the
SI rebrand happens: `interpretability.si` and `distill.si` are the ones that read
as real names with or without the thesis.
