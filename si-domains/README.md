# The .si bet — market read, where to buy, what to buy

Researched 2026-09-19. All prices USD unless noted. Availability was **not**
verified live (the research sandbox had no WHOIS/registrar egress) — run
`check_si.py` against `candidates.txt` before buying anything.

## 1. The tweet

Trump's Truth Social post (today) calls "Artificial Intelligence" *"inaccurate,
and very ineloquent"* and polls three replacements: **Superior Intelligence**,
**Extreme Intelligence**, **Supreme Intelligence**. ~30k votes in the first
hour, "Superior" leading.

Two of the three abbreviate to **SI**. Extreme → EI. So conditional on a rename
actually sticking, the poll itself is ~2-in-3 favorable to your thesis, and your
independent "synthetic intelligence" path lands on SI too. The thesis is sound.

## 2. The part that changes the trade: you are not early

This is not an untouched idea as of today. The .si/superintelligence trade has
been running since **May 2026**:

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| .si domains registered | ~186,159 (June 2026) |
| New registrations, trailing 30d | ~6,342 |
| Recursive.si | **$20,000** — bought by Recursive Superintelligence ($650M raise, $4.65B val) |
| Bot.si | **$20,000** — bought by a domain investor, not an end user |
| Military.si | $1,795 — end user (a Polish military-supply retailer) |

And the crucial number, from Domain Name Wire's June 11 analysis of 35 .si sales
between January and June 9: **only two were end users.** Nine of June's thirteen
sales went to a single investor in China (43% of public sales that year);
five more to one Singapore investor.

So the bid side of this market is, roughly, *two people*. Rising sale prices in
.si right now are mostly investors selling to investors. That is what a hype
cycle looks like from the inside, and it is the main reason to size this as
lottery tickets rather than as a position.

What today's tweet adds is a genuine new catalyst on top of an already-bid
market — which means the remaining edge is in vocabulary the May crowd had no
reason to buy, not in "superintelligence generics," which are gone or priced.

## 3. Where to buy — registrars

The registry is **Register.si**, run by ARNES (Slovenia's academic network).
Terms that matter:

- **No local presence, no restrictions.** Anyone, anywhere, individual or company.
- **Flat wholesale price: €10/yr.** No registry premium tiers, no per-name
  pricing. It was €8 from 2015 and €10 from 2005–2015 — this registry has
  resisted price increases for twenty years. Contrast .ai, which just went up $20.
  If SI branding takes off, the registry does **not** capture the upside. Early
  registrants do. That is the structurally attractive thing about this TLD.
- 2–63 characters; terms of 1–5 years; 30-day quarantine after expiry, then the
  name drops and is first-come-first-served (no registry auction).

Retail spread is enormous — about **10x** — so the single most concrete "deal"
here is simply not overpaying at the register:

| Registrar | Register | Renew | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cheapest tier (per TLD-List / tldes) | **~$11.45–11.50** | ~$11.51 | 39 registrars carry .si; sort the comparison table before each batch |
| Dynadot | $12.29 | $13.78 | Best practical pick: bulk search, bulk register, real API |
| EuroDNS / Regery / Instra / Marcaria / Let's Domains | ~$15–30 | — | fine, no reason to pay it |
| NEOSERV, Domains.si | local EUR pricing | — | Slovenian accredited registrars |
| **Gandi** | **$126** | — | **avoid** — 10x the market for the identical product |

Use Dynadot unless the comparison table shows something cheaper that day. There
is no product difference: .si has no registry premiums, so a name is either
€10-class or it is not available.

**Term length: buy 1-year, not 5.** Multi-year normally hedges price hikes, but
this registry hasn't raised prices in two decades, so the hike risk you'd be
hedging is small — and the option to *abandon* a speculative name after twelve
months is worth more than the hedge. On a 50-name basket that's the difference
between $600 at risk and $3,000.

## 4. Where to buy — aftermarket, and where you'd exit

- **Sedo** — deepest ccTLD support, built-in escrow, where most of the .si
  comps above cleared. Primary venue both directions.
- **Atom** — good seller tooling (interest-based price escalation).
- **Afternic** — best syndication reach (100+ registrar storefronts) but ccTLD
  support is patchier than Sedo's; list there second.
- **NamePros** — where the actual live .si bid currently sits, in the
  "SI Gold Rush" and ".si Play" threads. Worth reading before you buy; it's also
  a warning, because it means your exit is to the same people you're bidding against.
- **DomainMarket.si** — Slovenian marketplace, thin but local.

Be clear-eyed about the exit: ccTLD aftermarket sell-through is low
single-digit percent per year even in a hot extension. Assume you sell 1–3 names
out of 50, and that anything you hold you hold for years.

## 5. What to buy — where the edge actually is

Full list with rationale: `candidates.txt`. The ranking logic:

1. **Trump-rebrand vocabulary** — `superior`, `supreme`, and compounds. This is
   the only genuinely new information in the market today, and it has a shelf
   life measured in days. Highest-conviction tier purely on timing.
2. **"Synthetic intelligence"** — your own thesis, and the least crowded, because
   the May–June rush chased *super*, not *synthetic*. If the "artificial has bad
   connotations" rebrand happens on a longer horizon without Trump, this is the
   tier that pays.
3. **Domain hacks where `.si` completes the word** — `synthe.si` (synthesis),
   `autopoie.si` (autopoiesis), `homeosta.si`, `diagno.si`, `cataly.si`,
   `gene.si`, `the.si`. Underexploited: the rush bought `thing.si` generics, and
   hack names get valued on the whole string. Short stems are certainly gone;
   the long ones (`autopoie`, `homeosta`, `photosynthe`, `morphogene`) are the
   realistic hand-regs and read as genuine AI-company names.
4. **AI generics** — `latent`, `evals`, `guardrails`, `alignment`, `distill`.
   Check the leftovers, but the top of this tier is where the $20k comps are,
   and those comps were set by two investors. Do not chase them.
5. **Initialisms** (`asi`, `agi`, `si`) — listed only so the checker confirms
   they're gone rather than you wondering.

**Do not buy** existing-company marks (`ssi` = Safe Superintelligence, `openai`,
`anthropic`, `claude`). .si has an ARDS dispute procedure and those lose it.

## 6. A cheaper alternative to buying today

The May–June 2026 rush registered thousands of names on 1-year terms. Those
start expiring from **roughly May 2027**, then sit 30 days in quarantine and
**drop first-come-first-served — .si runs no registry auction for expiring
names.** A scripted catch at the drop, or a backorder service, gets you the
flippers' inventory at €10 instead of at their ask. If the hype has cooled by
then you'll have the pick of the basket; if it hasn't, you've lost nothing but
eight months of optionality.

## 7. How to actually execute, in order

1. `python3 check_si.py candidates.txt --out results.csv` (~3 min at 2s/query).
2. Take everything marked FREE in tiers 1–3, cross-check in a registrar cart.
3. Register in one batch at Dynadot or the cheapest lister that day, **1-year terms**.
4. Cap the basket. 40–60 names ≈ $500–750/yr all-in is a sane lottery ticket;
   past ~100 names the renewal carry starts outrunning any plausible hit rate.
5. List the keepers on Sedo at make-offer, and set a calendar note for
   May 2027 for the drop pool.

## 8. What would falsify the thesis

- Trump's poll winner turns out to be "Extreme Intelligence" (→ EI, not SI).
- The rename gets no adoption beyond one post — the base rate for presidential
  coinages sticking to an industry term is low.
- End-user demand stays at two buyers per half-year while investor inventory
  keeps growing. Watch DNW's end-user sales reports: the tell is *end users*
  buying .si, not investors flipping to each other.
- ARNES changes policy. Non-profit registries have imposed anti-speculation
  rules before. There was no such announcement as of today.
